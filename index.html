Catch diabetic eye damage before vision is lost.

DRishti helps health workers screen diabetic patients near their homes. Take a retina photo with a phone, a 20D lens and an adapter, add a few health readings, and get a risk level with advice in Kannada.

How it works

01

Capture

Phone camera with a 20D lens held by an adapter. A trained person dilates the pupil.

02

Upload

Pick the sharpest frame. The site checks for blur, glare and darkness.

03

Analyse

The image grade is combined with sugar, blood pressure and years of diabetes.

04

Refer

A risk level and advice appear in Kannada. A doctor confirms every referral.

Why it matters

Indians live with diabetes (ICMR-INDIAB, 2023).

16.9%
